## About Me

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Natural & Applied Sciences at TERI School of Advanced Studies, New Delhi.

My research lies at the intersection of **geospatial data science, remote sensing, GIS, machine learning, deep learning, Earth system science, groundwater systems and environmental modelling**.

My doctoral research focuses on the assessment and prediction of groundwater contamination using multi-source environmental and Earth observation datasets, with current work on **fluoride, uranium and arsenic**.

My broader research interests extend toward **cryosphere-influenced hydrology**, including snow, permafrost, glacier-fed systems, terrestrial water storage and the relationships between cryospheric processes and surface–subsurface water availability.

## Research Highlights

**Fluoride**  
Comparing and benchmarking deep-learning models for predicting groundwater contamination and investigating the effects of spatial resolution on prediction.

**Uranium**  
Predictive modelling and spatial risk mapping of uranium-contaminated groundwater in India using automated machine learning.

**Spatial Downscaling**  
Development of an ML-based methodology for downscaling coarse environmental datasets.

**Cryosphere**  
Exploring the role of snow, permafrost and glacier-fed systems in terrestrial water and groundwater processes.
