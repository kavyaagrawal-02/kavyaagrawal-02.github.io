Research experience

  • 2025 – 26
    92U

    Uranium in groundwater across India

    A national-scale model of where uranium occurs in India's groundwater, built from groundwater observations and environmental predictors and checked against an independent validation dataset. The predictions are turned into spatial risk classes, estimates of population exposure, and governance indicators showing where monitoring and action are most needed.

    Methods: automated machine learning (AutoGluon), independent validation, spatial risk mapping, exposure assessment.

    Paper submitted to journal; Best Paper Award, ICSSR EHP NEXUS 2026
  • 2026 –
    9F

    Fluoride and the effect of spatial resolution

    India-wide prediction of fluoride contamination, benchmarking deep learning models and testing how the resolution of environmental predictors changes the result. Predictor stacks are built in Google Earth Engine at native resolution and at 500 m, 1 km and 5 km, keeping every groundwater observation in the data.

    Methods: deep learning, multi-resolution predictor extraction, 1.5 mg/L threshold.

    In progress
  • 2026 –
    DS

    Downscaling and upscaling environmental predictors

    A physically informed framework for moving satellite data between coarse and fine resolutions without inventing detail. A pilot on CHIRPS rainfall over Punjab (2013–2022) targets 5 km, 1 km and 500 m using sub-pixel fractions and conservation-based aggregation.

    Methods: machine learning, conservation-based aggregation; validated with Moran's I, semivariograms and aggregation consistency.

    In progress
  • 2024 – 25
    33As

    Arsenic in the Middle Gangetic Plain

    The distribution, speciation and controls on arsenic mobilisation in the aquifers of the Middle Gangetic Plain, looking at geogenic and hydrogeochemical factors such as pH, iron, redox potential and sulfate.

    Methods: hydrogeochemistry, explainable AI (SHAP, LIME).

    Presented at As2024, Bhubaneswar
  • 2023
    LST

    Land surface temperature trends across the Indus River Basin

    Spatial and elevation-wise trends in daytime and nighttime land surface temperature across the Indus River Basin.

    Methods: Google Earth Engine (JavaScript), ArcGIS, Mann–Kendall and Theil–Sen trend analysis.

    Published, Journal of Mountain Science
  • 2023

    Identification and mapping of permafrost in the Indus Basin

    Mapping likely permafrost from about twenty years of mean annual air temperature (MAAT), potential incoming solar radiation (PISR), BMAT, snow and precipitation estimates in Google Earth Engine.

  • 2023

    Snow cover and changes in Indian basins
